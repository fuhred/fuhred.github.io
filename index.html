I'm Fred Duong, a researcher studying how Americans understand politics, identity, and each other.

I am a Senior Research Manager at More in Common US, where I lead nationally representative research on polarization and social cohesion in America. My work spans survey design, segmentation, qualitative methods, and R-based data analysis, producing findings that are rigorous, accessible, and visually compelling.

I have an M.A. from New York University, a Ph.D. in psychology from Northeastern University, and postdoctoral training at the University of Toronto.
